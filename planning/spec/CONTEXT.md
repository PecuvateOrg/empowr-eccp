# Product Specification

This area defines what ECCP must deliver and how completion will be judged.

## Product Goal

Create an accessible pathway that helps eligible people understand, undertake, and evidence Empowr coaching certification while giving authorised staff clear programme oversight.

## Known Programme Shape

1. Foundation — Empowr principles, programme knowledge, and facilitation basics
2. Practitioner — deeper facilitation skills, session planning, and member wellbeing
3. Advanced Coach — programme design, community leadership, and coaching facilitators

## MVP Scope

The MVP remains to be agreed with Empowr. Candidate capabilities requiring confirmation include applicant onboarding, curriculum delivery, progress tracking, evidence submission, assessment, certification, and staff administration.

## Initial Acceptance Criteria

- The programme's three levels and progression are represented accurately.
- Member, practitioner, assessor, and administrator permissions are explicitly defined before protected features are built.
- Certification status cannot be mistaken for an operational role appointment.
- Personal data and evidence storage have documented retention, access, and deletion rules before collection begins.
- Core journeys meet responsive and accessibility quality floors before release.
